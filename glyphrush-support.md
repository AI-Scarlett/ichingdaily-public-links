## Common Questions

### Does Glyph Rush require payment?

No. Glyph Rush does not use paid unlocks, subscriptions, or in-app purchases. Content unlocks through gameplay progression.

### Does Glyph Rush collect personal data?

No personal data is collected by the app. Progress and settings are stored locally on the device. If Game Center is enabled by the player, Apple handles Game Center account and leaderboard data.

### How do I reset progress?

Open Settings in Glyph Rush and use Reset Data to clear local progress.

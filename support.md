## Purchases and Subscriptions

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. To manage or cancel a subscription:

1. Open the Settings app on your Apple device.
2. Tap your Apple ID.
3. Tap Subscriptions.
4. Select JadeName: Chinese Names.

## Restore Purchases

Open the app, go to Settings, and choose Restore Purchases.

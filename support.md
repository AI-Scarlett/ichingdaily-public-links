## Common Topics

## Purchases and Subscriptions

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. To manage or cancel a subscription:

1. Open the Settings app on your Apple device.
2. Tap your Apple ID.
3. Tap Subscriptions.
4. Select I Ching Daily: Oracle & Wisdom.

## Restore Purchases

Open the app, go to Settings, and choose Restore Purchases.

## AI Features

AI features are optional and require a user-provided API key. If AI is disabled, the core offline reading features still work.

# Glyph Rush Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 25, 2026

Glyph Rush is an offline reflex puzzle game for spotting subtle Chinese character forms. The app does not show third-party ads, does not use third-party analytics, does not track users, and does not sell paid unlocks.

## Data Stored on Device

Glyph Rush stores gameplay progress, best score, settings, completed run count, and weak glyph records locally on the device. This information is used only to unlock content through play and improve the player's practice loop.

## Game Center

If the player signs in to Game Center, Apple may process Game Center account information, leaderboard scores, achievements, and related gameplay data according to Apple's terms and privacy practices. Glyph Rush uses Game Center only for player authentication, leaderboards, and future achievements.

## Purchases and Ads

Glyph Rush does not include in-app purchases, subscriptions, paid unlocks, third-party advertising, or tracking SDKs. All content unlocks through gameplay progression.

## Deleting Data

Players can delete local gameplay data from the Settings screen by using Reset Data. Game Center data is managed through the player's Apple account and Apple's Game Center settings.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the support page linked on the App Store product page.
